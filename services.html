FAQ

Things clients ask before we start.

Do you work solo or with a team?

Solo for most engagements — that's why I keep scope tight and senior. For larger builds I bring in a trusted network of specialists (BI, ML ops, infra) that I lead directly.

What stacks do you work with?

Azure Data, Databricks, Snowflake, dbt, Python, SQL, Power BI, Streamlit. If you're on AWS or GCP, I'll tell you honestly whether the job is a good fit.

How are engagements priced?

Fixed-scope, fixed-fee for productized engagements. Retainers for fractional leadership. No hourly billing — you pay for outcomes.

What does "handover" actually mean?

Full documentation, CI, runbooks, tests, and a named owner on your side. If your team can't run the system a month after I leave, I haven't done my job.

When can you start?

Usually within 2–4 weeks of the scoping call. I take 1–2 engagements in parallel to stay senior on every one.

Not sure which engagement fits?

Book a 30-minute call. I'll diagnose the system that will move your business furthest — and tell you honestly if it's not something I should build.
